## 2. Capturing Smooth 60fps Animations for Behance

Behance supports **GIFs** (up to 50MB) and **embedded MP4/Vimeo videos** for ultra-crisp motion.

### A. Recommended Free Tools
1. **ScreenToGif (Windows):** Free lightweight tool. Set capture framerate to **60 FPS**, select the MacBook viewport on your screen, record 1 full loop (10-15s), and export as GIF or MP4.
2. **OBS Studio:** Record full-screen at 1920x1080 @ 60fps in pristine 4K/1080p MP4.
3. **Cap / Kap / Cleanshot:** For clean window-focused recording with smooth cursor hiding.

### B. Micro-Interactions to Record
1. **MacBook Pro Auto-Scroll:**
   - Open [NEXTFORM Behance Mode](https://promi-15.github.io/Nexform-Website/#behance).
   - The desktop mockup automatically scrolls through the website at smooth 60fps.
2. **Hybrid Assist Mode Switcher:**
   - Click between `Human Only`, `Hybrid Assist`, and `Machine Assist` to record the interactive HUD gauge moving from `0%` to `58%` to `94%`.
3. **Live Telemetry Waveform:**
   - The heart rate / muscle telemetry oscillates in real-time with responsive crimson signal bars.
